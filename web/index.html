Thank you to AgilePredict for making its forecasting code available. Our model builds substantially on that work, adapting parts of the published modelling code and adding models and adjustments. This project primarily explores presenting forecasts in a format that’s easy to read at a glance, rather than claiming better forecast accuracy. In historical tests, accuracy was similar to our reproduction of its model; this does not establish how we compare with its live service.

This experimental service provides estimates, not confirmed future prices. Updates may be late or missing, and errors in source data or model assumptions can affect the results. We make no guarantees about the service’s availability or the accuracy, completeness or suitability of its outputs. Check published prices with your supplier and use other information alongside these forecasts when making financial, operational or energy-use decisions.
