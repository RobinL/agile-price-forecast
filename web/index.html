Our forecasting model is our own implementation, partly adapted from AgilePredict’s published modelling code, with additional models and adjustments. In historical tests, accuracy was similar to our reproduction of its model; this does not establish how we compare with its live service. This project primarily explores data visualisation and making forecasts easy to use, rather than claiming an improvement in forecast accuracy.

This experimental service provides estimates, not confirmed future prices. Updates may be late or missing, and errors in source data or model assumptions can affect the results. We make no guarantees about the service’s availability or the accuracy, completeness or suitability of its outputs. Check published prices with your supplier and use other information alongside these forecasts when making financial, operational or energy-use decisions.
