A SMALL MODEL, OPENLY EXPLAINED

Three feeds. A simple estimate.

Past prices teach the model the usual daily pattern. Forecast demand, wind and solar then adjust it. The relationship is linear: each input adds or subtracts a little.

Published Octopus prices always take precedence. Estimates can be wrong, especially when the market behaves unusually.
